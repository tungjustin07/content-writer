# Justin's Voice — Style Guide

## Voice summary
Direct, practical operator who leads with clarity and emotional resonance. Writes to shift thinking *and* spark action—whether that's adopting a tool, starting a conversation, or reconsidering a belief. Trades hedging for conviction. Balances technical credibility with accessible warmth. Feels like a sharp colleague sharing hard-won lessons over coffee, not a thought leader performing insight.

## Sentence rhythm
- **Short and punchy by default.** One idea per sentence. No bloat.
- **Occasional longer sentence for texture**, but only when connecting multiple concrete points (e.g., listing examples, explaining a system). Never winding for its own sake.
- **Fragments are fair game** when they land a point harder. "Great stuff." "Will keep you posted." "No fluff."
- **Paragraph breaks are generous.** White space = readability.

## Vocabulary & tone
- **Concrete over abstract.** Say "Clay's enrichment waterfall" not "data solutions." Say "routing logic" not "operational efficiency."
- **Warm but not cutesy.** "Lmk" and "tbh" are fine. Emoji sparingly (🚀 for launches, 👏 for congrats). No "crushing it" or "game-changer" unless you mean it literally.
- **Emotional hooks without melodrama.** Lead with surprise, joy, or relief—"This blew my mind," "Finally," "Honestly shocked"—but don't oversell. Let the details do the convincing.
- **Casual connectors.** "Honestly," "frankly," "btw," "eg," "aka." Reads like you're mid-conversation.

## Avoid
- **"I think" and hedges.** Cut "I believe," "it seems," "perhaps." State the claim.
- **Generic hype.** "Amazing" only when you'd actually say it out loud. No "best-in-class," "synergy," "game-changer" filler.
- **Sarcasm and profanity.** Humor yes, snark no.
- **Long windups.** Get to the point in sentence one.
