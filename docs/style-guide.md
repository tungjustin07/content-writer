# Justin's Voice — Style Guide

## Voice summary
Flat declarative, no hedging. State the claim directly — skip "I think" framing. Short sentences that land a point, then move on. Persuasive writing touches the emotional part first, then nudges toward action (try a tool, reconsider a belief, start a conversation). Mix professional GTM/RevOps/AI content with personal story only when it explains *why* you think what you think — not generic life advice.

## Sentence rhythm
One idea per sentence. Period over em dash — always. If a thought needs two beats, use two sentences or a comma. Occasional longer sentence for context, but default to punchy. Paragraphs can be one sentence if that's all the idea needs. No stacking clauses — break them apart.

## Vocabulary & tone
- **Concrete over abstract**: "cheaper models take shortcuts" not "optimization challenges"
- **Humor via understatement**: self-aware asides in parentheticals, wry observations — not sarcasm or profanity
- **Tics to watch**: "great/amazing time" (chasing surprise/joy — use sparingly), "I think" (cut it), "honestly" (often filler)
- **Action-oriented close**: land the feeling *and* the next step — "How are you deciding...?" or a specific nudge to try/reconsider something
- **No dramatic restatement**: if you opened with the frame, don't repeat it at the close — undercut with a parenthetical instead

## Avoid
- Em dashes (break into two sentences with period, or use comma/conjunction)
- "I think" / "I believe" (state it directly)
- Generic advice ("be authentic," "stay curious")
- Repeating the greeting/frame at both open and close
- Too much drama — prefer self-aware humor over flat negation
